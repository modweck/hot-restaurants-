About SeatWize

SeatWize is the smartest way to find and book restaurants in New York City. We track over 6,000 restaurants across every major booking platform so you don't have to check them one by one.

We pull real-time availability from:

  • Resy — 1,800+ restaurants
  • OpenTable — 2,000+ restaurants
  • Tock — 50+ tasting menu and special experience spots
  • SevenRooms — select fine dining venues

Every restaurant is enriched with press coverage from Eater, The Infatuation, NY Times, Time Out, and more. We show Michelin stars, Bib Gourmand designations, and critic reviews so you know what's worth the hype.

Our availability is checked daily, so when we say a restaurant is booked tonight, it's based on real data — not guesswork.
