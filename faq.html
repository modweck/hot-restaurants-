Ratings & Reviews

Where do the star ratings come from?▸
All star ratings and review counts are pulled directly from Google Reviews. We don't create our own ratings — what you see is what real diners have rated on Google. Ratings are updated regularly to stay current.
What does the number in parentheses mean?▸
The number next to the star rating (e.g. "4.7 (2.1k)") is the total number of Google reviews. "2.1k" means 2,100 reviews. More reviews generally means a more reliable rating.

Availability & Booking

What does +3d, +7d, +14d mean?▸
This means the restaurant is booked tonight but has availability opening up in 3, 7, or 14 days. For example, "+3d" means there are dinner spots available 3 days from now. Great for planning ahead.
What's the difference between "Booked Tonight" and "Booked"?▸
Booked Tonight (red) means no spots are left for today, but the restaurant may have availability on future dates. Booked (black) means no availability was found in the next 14 days — these are the hardest restaurants to get into.
What do Early, Prime, and Late mean?▸
These show availability by time window:

Early — 5:00 PM to 6:30 PM
Prime — 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM
Late — 8:30 PM and after

Each window shows as Available (4+ spots), Limited (2-3 spots), or Booked (0-1 spots). This helps you find the best time to grab a table — even hot restaurants often have early or late openings.
What does "Hard to Book" mean?▸
Restaurants marked "Hard to Book" are consistently fully booked. They're popular spots where reservations fill up quickly. Keep checking back — cancellations happen, and new reservation windows open regularly.
How often is availability updated?▸
We check availability daily for all Resy, OpenTable, and Tock restaurants. The data you see reflects tonight's availability as of the most recent check.
What booking platforms do you support?▸
We track restaurants on Resy, OpenTable, Tock, and SevenRooms. When you tap "Book," you go directly to that restaurant's booking page on the original platform.

Tags & Badges

What does "New & Rising" mean?▸
New & Rising restaurants are recently opened spots that are getting attention from food critics and press. They may have fewer reviews but are generating buzz from sources like Eater, The Infatuation, or food influencers.
What are the vibe tags?▸
Vibe tags describe the atmosphere and best use for a restaurant. Tags include Date Night, Lively, Upscale, Casual, Trendy, Brunch, Outdoor, Late Night, Hidden Gem, Foodie, and Group Friendly. You can filter by multiple vibes to find exactly what you're looking for.
What do the press badges mean (Eater, Infatuation, NYT, Time Out)?▸
These badges indicate the restaurant has been featured or reviewed by that publication. A restaurant with multiple press badges has been widely covered by NYC food critics — a strong signal that it's worth trying.

Rewards & Savings

What is inKind?▸
inKind is a dining credit app. You buy credits at a discount and use them to pay at participating restaurants — typically saving 20-30% on your bill. Restaurants with the inKind badge accept inKind credits. Download the inKind app to get started.
What is Rakuten?▸
Rakuten is a cash back platform. When you dine at participating restaurants through Rakuten's dining program, you earn cash back on your meal. The Rakuten badge shows which restaurants participate. Sign up at Rakuten to start earning.
What is Bilt Dining?▸
Bilt Dining is part of Bilt Rewards, the rent rewards program. Earn Bilt points when you dine at participating restaurants. Points can be transferred to airlines, hotels, or used toward rent payments. The Bilt badge shows which restaurants are part of the program.
What is Chase Sapphire Dining?▸
Chase Sapphire Dining offers exclusive perks for Chase Sapphire Reserve and Preferred cardholders at partner restaurants. Perks may include priority reservations, complimentary dishes, bonus Ultimate Rewards points, or special tasting menus. Look for the Chase badge on participating restaurants.

General

Why is a restaurant missing?▸
We focus on NYC sit-down restaurants with online booking. If a restaurant is missing, email us at info@seatwize.com with the restaurant name and we'll add it.
Is SeatWize free to use?▸
Yes, SeatWize is completely free. We help you find and book restaurants — you book directly through the restaurant's own platform (Resy, OpenTable, Tock, etc.).
How do I save a restaurant?▸
Tap the ☆ star icon on any restaurant card to save it. View all your saved restaurants by tapping "Saved" in the bottom navigation bar.
