Overview

SeatWize ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

Account information: When you sign up, we collect your email address and authentication data through Supabase (our authentication provider). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from your Google account.

Usage data: We collect information about how you use SeatWize, including restaurants you search for, save, and view. This helps us improve the service.

Location data: If you grant permission, we use your location to show nearby restaurants and calculate distances. Location data is used in real-time and not stored on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the SeatWize service
  • To personalize your experience (saved restaurants, preferences)
  • To calculate distances to restaurants
  • To improve our restaurant data and recommendations
  • To communicate service updates if necessary

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect payment information — all bookings happen on third-party platforms (Resy, OpenTable, Tock, etc.)
  • We do not track your browsing activity outside of SeatWize
  • We do not use advertising trackers

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Period.

We use the following services to operate SeatWize:

  • Supabase — Authentication and user accounts
  • Google Maps — Map display and distance calculations
  • Netlify — Website hosting

These services have their own privacy policies and process data only as needed to provide their services to us.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to:

  • Keep you logged in (authentication session)
  • Store your saved restaurants
  • Cache search results for faster loading

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies.

Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as you have an active account. Saved restaurants are stored in your browser's local storage. You can delete your saved restaurants at any time by removing them in the app.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Opt out of any future communications

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@seatwize.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
